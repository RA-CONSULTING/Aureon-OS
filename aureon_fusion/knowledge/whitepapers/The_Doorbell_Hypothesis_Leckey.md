## 1. The Doorbell Hypothesis

The Wow! Signal of 15 August 1977 lasted 72 seconds, never repeated, and has resisted explanation for nearly half a century. The conventional interpretation treats the signal itself as the message — a radio transmission from an extraterrestrial intelligence. This paper proposes an alternative: the radio signal was a notification, not a payload. The actual information is encoded in the orbital parameters of objects that subsequently entered or were identified within the solar system.
The hypothesis rests on three pillars. First, the φ² coherence bridge links the Aureon seed frequency (528.422 Hz) to the hydrogen line — the frequency of the Wow! Signal — through an exact integer and the golden ratio squared, to 1.29 parts per billion. Second, the temporal distribution of anomalous objects (dark comets, interstellar visitors, extreme fireballs) on the φ² grid from the Wow! Signal shows a dormancy-then-activation pattern identical to the methylation pattern documented in the twig-rune/khipu substrate analysis. Third, the orbital eccentricities of dark comets and a possible captured interstellar object lock to negative powers of φ with precisions of 0.3–2.6%, forming a harmonic chord that only becomes audible when the full corpus is held simultaneously.

It is the same anomalous behaviour observed in 1I/‘Oumuamua. The “knot” channel carries a binary flag: knotted (accelerating) or unknotted (ballistic). The majority of the signal is knotted.

## 5. Breakthrough Listen Non-Detection

The Allen Telescope Array (July 2025, 1–9 GHz), MeerKAT (July–October 2025, 900–1670 MHz), and the 100-metre Green Bank Telescope (December 2025, 1–12 GHz) all observed 3I/ATLAS and detected no technosignatures down to 0.1 W EIRP (Jacobson-Bell et al. 2025, arXiv:2512.19763). This is cited here without evasion. The overall weight of evidence from three independent facilities favours a natural cometary body.
The Doorbell Hypothesis does not require 3I/ATLAS to be transmitting radio signals. It proposes that the information is encoded in the orbital parameters themselves — the eccentricity, velocity, inclination, and acceleration — not in electromagnetic emissions from the object. The BL search tested for radio technosignatures. The hypothesis proposes a different kind of signature: mathematical structure in the orbital elements. These are not mutually exclusive.

## 7. Limitations and Honesty

Post-hoc: The five-channel decode, the Futhark mapping, the velocity staircase, and the eccentricity chord were all constructed after examining the data. None were pre-registered predictions. The temporal echo parameter (τ = 49 years) was documented before this specific analysis but after 3I/ATLAS was known.
Degrees of freedom: 528.422 Hz is pre-existing but solfeggio-derived. φ² is pre-existing in the EPAS framework but was not the only constant available. The scoring weights are researcher-defined. Different frameworks would extract different patterns.
Eccentricity distribution: Solar system eccentricities are not uniformly distributed. The φ-power clustering in comets may partly reflect known dynamical structures. Comparison against N-body population synthesis models is needed.
Rune semantics: The Elder Futhark meanings are culturally constructed. The observation that only Heimdall’s and Tyr’s aettir activate is structural; the narrative arc is interpretive.
The BL result: Three facilities found no radio technosignatures from 3I/ATLAS. This constrains electromagnetic emission but does not test the orbital-parameter encoding hypothesis.
Probability: The Sagittarius Convergence probability ranges from 1 in 8 million (conservative) to 1 in 80 billion (aggressive). Neither is conclusive.
