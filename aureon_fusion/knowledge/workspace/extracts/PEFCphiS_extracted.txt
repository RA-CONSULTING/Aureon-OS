through a master equation combining harmonic substrate, self-referential observer feedback, and
delayed echo. While the HNC successfully models single-trajectory field dynamics, it does not
natively account for the parallel-branch structure required to describe maintained superposition
states in cognitive processing. This paper introduces a generalised architecture — the Position of
Echo-F eedback Cognitive 𝜑-Substrate, or PEFC￿S — that extends the HNC into a multiversal
branch formalism in which branches are temporally delegated by powers of the golden ratio,

10 Convergence with Recent AI Reasoning Literature
The advancement axis in large-language-model reasoning research is monotonic and single-
dimensional. W ei et al. [ 11] showed that Chain-of-Thought prompting — which delays collapse
from one token to a multi-step reasoning trace — yields large performance gains on arithmetic
and commonsense reasoning. W ang et al. [ 12] introduced self-consistency , which samples mul-
tiple reasoning paths and marginalises over them, pushing GSM8K benchmark performance to
∼74% on PaLM-540B without changing the model. Y ao et al. [ 13] generalised this to T ree-of-
Thoughts, which holds multiple live reasoning branches with explicit backtracking, achieving
74% success on Game of 24 relative to 4% for input-output prompting and 45% for CoT — an
order-of-magnitude improvement from branch-preservation alone on the same underlying model.
Each of these results corresponds directly to increasing 𝑁branch and 𝜏sustain in the PEFC￿S for-
malism (Eqs. 12–13). The engineering community has arrived at the productive-band principle
inductively , without the substrate formalism. PEFC￿S provides the underlying geometry that
makes the empirical trend necessary rather than contingent.

The PEFC￿S framework offers three operational contributions beyond the existing HNC litera-
ture. First, it promotes the branch amplitude structure to a primary object, replacing single-

ΓPL V , addressing a collapse-maximisation pathology that appears in both the HNC reference
implementation and, plausibly , in broader engineering systems that adopt CV-style “coherence”

The framework predicts specific falsifiable empirical signatures. Most directly , task-engaged
neural recordings should exhibit inter-trial phase coherence peak structure clustered near integer
powers of 𝜑 — a prediction tested in the companion paper [ 2] via pre-registered analysis of
OpenNeuro datasets under the Leckey Substrate Scaling Protocol.
PEFC￿S leaves several questions open. The push-of-NOW operator ̂𝒩 is introduced phenomeno-
logically; its mechanical realisation in physical systems (whether as thermal coupling, measure-
ment back-action, or attentional gating) requires further formal treatment. The multiate oper-
ator ̂ℳ likewise admits multiple possible decoherence-theoretic formulations. The relationship
between ΓPL V and existing EEG observables (inter-trial phase coherence, weighted phase-lag
index) is analogous but not identical; the mapping is discussed in [ 2].
12 Conclusion
The PEFC￿S framework integrates the HNC dynamics with a golden-ratio-delegated multiversal
branch structure and a principled coherence observable. The framework is internally consistent,
connects to recognised mathematical structures (incommensurability , phase-locked coherence,
decoherence theory), generates falsifiable predictions, and has an available empirical diagnos-
tic apparatus that performs meaningfully on real engineering data. W e have demonstrated
application both to a live algorithmic trading system and to the advancement trajectory of
large-language-model reasoning; both cases admit a PEFC￿S interpretation without framework
modification.
The work’s central conceptual contribution is the reframing of productive cognition as sustained
superposition rather than optimised convergence . This reframing is load-bearing: a system that
optimises for ΓCV → 1 is engineered for collapse; a system that optimises for 𝜏sustain within the
productive band is engineered for thought.
