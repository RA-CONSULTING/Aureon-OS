The firn→bedrock and water→bedrock interfaces are strong acoustic reflectors — exactly what harmonic LIDAR is designed to detect. A subglacial water body (lake or saturated zone) at the candidate would produce a +0.82 reflection (67% energy return) at the water/bedrock interface.
