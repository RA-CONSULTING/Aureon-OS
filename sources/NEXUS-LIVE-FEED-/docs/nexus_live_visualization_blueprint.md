## Layered Visualization Suite
The experience unfolds across ten interlinked visualization layers. Each layer can stand alone yet synchronizes with the others through the Nexus Command Center.

### 1. Planetary Heartbeat — Gaia's Pulse
- **Form**: A breathing 3D Earth with atmospheric shaders, Schumann resonance-driven pulsations (~7.83 Hz), and meditation-triggered ripples.
- **Data Inputs**: Schumann resonance baselines, global coherence index (Γ), collective emotional sentiment, live meditation gatherings.
- **Encoding**: Color gradient from deep red (Γ ≤ 0.2) to violet/gold (Γ ≥ 0.945), brightness tracks coherence intensity, storm vortices depict stress zones.
- **Impact**: Users witness the planet "breathing" and see their meditation contribute light.

- **Default**: Planetary Heartbeat center (40%), Frequency Spectrum top-left, Domain Wheel top-right, Timeline River bottom-left, Consciousness Map bottom-right.
- **Presets**: Scientist (Spectrum, Entanglement Web, Zero-Point Flow), Meditator (Heartbeat, Consciousness Map, Emotional Weather), Futurist (Timeline River, Domain Wheel, Akashic Interface).

- **Engine**: Unity 3D or Unreal Engine 5 with WebGL support.
- **Data Pipeline**: Quantum sensors → Nexus Core → WebSocket stream → visualization clients.
