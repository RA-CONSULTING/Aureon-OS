Controller

R&A Consulting and Brokerage Services Ltd, company number NI696693, is responsible for the Aureon Zorza Technologies website and company correspondence described here.

Website data

This is a public information website. It does not provide public user accounts, payment processing or public comments. The site does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies.

The hosting service may create technical server logs, such as an IP address, request time, requested address, browser identifier and response status, to deliver, secure and diagnose the service. Retention and access are limited by the hosting account settings and applicable law.

Correspondence

If you choose to contact the company, the information may include your name, role, organisation, contact details, message, attachments and the resulting correspondence. It is used to read, route and respond to the matter, maintain an appropriate business record, establish or perform an agreement, comply with a legal duty and protect the company’s legitimate interests. Consent is used where it is the appropriate basis. Do not send sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for the matter.

The website does not use personal data for solely automated decisions or behavioural advertising. A person remains responsible for consequential company decisions.

Sharing, transfers and retention

Personal data is shared only where necessary with service providers that support hosting, email, document storage or security; professional advisers; a party involved in a legitimate company transaction; or a public authority where law requires it. Service providers act under their own terms and applicable data-protection duties.

If a service provider processes personal data outside the United Kingdom, the company uses the transfer mechanism and safeguards made available for that service and reviews the relevant account settings. No personal data is sold through this website.

Technical logs and correspondence are kept only for as long as needed for service security, the purpose of the exchange, an active relationship, accounting or legal obligations, or the establishment and defence of legal claims. Records are reviewed and removed or restricted when those reasons no longer apply.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data-protection law may give you rights to ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability. Requests are assessed against the legal basis and any record-keeping duty that applies.

You may also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office ↗. The company would appreciate the opportunity to investigate the concern first.
