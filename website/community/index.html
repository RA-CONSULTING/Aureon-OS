How we document support

Respect the partner. Preserve the proof.

A community entry is published only when there is an original company record, partner acknowledgement, public programme record or permission-cleared image. That prevents a logo, a social mention or an informal conversation from being overstated as a partnership claim.

  1. 01
    Identify the contribution

    State the support or activity plainly, without claiming the partner’s outcomes.

  2. 02
    Link the original record

    Use the partner, council or company source so the context can be checked.

  3. 03
    Credit images and marks

    Use company-supplied or permission-cleared media and keep third-party ownership visible.
