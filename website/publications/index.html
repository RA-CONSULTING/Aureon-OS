Five labels for reading the opportunity.

Each label answers a practical question: what can be inspected today, and what would materially strengthen the case next?

Company-built

Independently reviewed

How to read the research

Four questions for investor review.

The aim is not to count publications. It is to identify research that can sharpen the product, open a category or create defensible option value.

  1. 01
    Identify the questionLook for a consequential problem with a clear reason to matter.
  2. 02
    Inspect the methodAsk whether the proposition is coherent and falsifiable.
  3. 03
    Connect it to valueFind the platform capability, sector wedge or option it may strengthen.
  4. 04
    Name the next validationChoose the experiment or independent review that would change conviction.
