Company-built reading model

HNC proposes the grammar. Aureon OS runs the system. Evidence OS controls the claim.

HNC organises evolving questions around signal, integration, memory and feedback. Aureon OS turns that direction into an inspectable local-first software organism. Evidence OS is the reusable provenance, claim-state, human-authority and review plane carried through every application.

Layer 03 / Authority required

Show the open gate before asking for a decision.

A named human reviewer retains authority. Independent technical review, production controls and outcome validation remain open and are not inferred from repository access.

Stop condition
Identity, permission, evidence quality or decision authority is unresolved
Reviewer receives
A clear approve, revise or decline choice with no default approval
Layer 04 / Approval bounded

Release only what the evidence and authority support.

The bounded output is a publicly scoped review record linking the source, claim state, human gate and unresolved independent-review requirement.

What enters
The recorded human decision and the exact approved public scope
System action

Stop condition
The output exceeds the source, approval or disclosure boundary
Reviewer receives
A reproducible record that can be reopened and challenged
