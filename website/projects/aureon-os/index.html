Platform architecture

One software organism. One cross-cutting control plane.

Evidence OS carries four accountable layers across Aureon OS: permitted sources, provenance and claim state, human review, and bounded outputs. Select a layer to inspect what it carries forward, what stops the workflow and what a reviewer can verify.

The view is an operating-model and public-architecture explanation. It is not evidence that every connector, control or workflow is production-deployed.

Evidence OS operating contract

Four rules the interface should not hide.

The control-plane proposition is strongest when the boundary is legible before a buyer or reviewer asks for it.

01 / Local-first

Keep sensitive context close.

Use public, redacted or explicitly permitted evidence for public views. Credentials, personal data and restricted control surfaces remain private.

02 / Source-linked

Make the record challengeable.

Material statements retain a source, evidence state and interpretation boundary so another reviewer can re-open the basis rather than trust a summary.

03 / Human-gated

Separate assistance from authority.

Automation may gather, classify and prepare. A sensitive submission, filing, payment, deployment or representation remains a human decision.

04 / Review-retained

Keep the decision trail inspectable.

Important actions retain their source, authority and read-back. Drafts, staged material and completed outcomes stay distinct.
