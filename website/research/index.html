01 / Question & scope

State the proposition so it can be challenged.

Define the research question, intended domain, assumptions, exclusions and the decision the work is meant to inform. A broad idea is not silently converted into a company capability.

Carries forward
A bounded question, named author or owner, and a versioned scope.
Stops when
The proposition is too broad to test or depends on an unstated assumption.
Reviewer opens
The stated scope, programme identity and direct source trail.
02 / Record & provenance

Identify what was actually published.

Record the title, author, source platform, record type, DOI where available and current link state. Concept DOIs and record DOIs are not counted as separate works.

Carries forward
A unique public record tied to its source, type and interpretation boundary.
Stops when
A URL resolves to another record, attribution is unclear or the record cannot be inspected.
Reviewer opens
Zenodo, ResearchGate, ORCID, Substack or the public repository.
03 / Test & challenge

Separate a proposed mechanism from measured evidence.

Define the test method, comparison condition, acceptance threshold, data lineage and reviewer. In this register, source-linked research is not represented as independently validated.

Carries forward
A falsifiable test plan, results record and named evidence state.
Stops when
The method, baseline, raw evidence or independent challenge is missing.
Reviewer opens
The method, limitations, result trace and unresolved questions.
04 / Review & translation

Translate only the evidence that survived review.

A human reviewer decides whether a result can inform a technical programme, funding route or product hypothesis. Public visibility is not endorsement or deployment proof.

Carries forward
A bounded conclusion, review receipt and explicit next proof.
Stops when
The proposed product, partner or performance claim exceeds the reviewed evidence.
Reviewer opens
The decision basis, authority, limitations and follow-up gate.

Translation rule: no capability, product, partner, funder or performance conclusion advances until its supporting evidence and human authority are visible.

Record policy

Traceable sources. Conservative interpretation.

Preprints, working papers and platform records are not peer-reviewed publications unless their source explicitly says so. The repository also contains working material; its files are not individually presented as independently verified publications.

Automated access to ResearchGate may be provider-restricted. That access restriction is recorded rather than treated as a missing or independently verified source.
