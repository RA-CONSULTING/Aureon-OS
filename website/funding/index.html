Validation path

Move from relevance to evidence, then to the next proof.

The sequence keeps the public proposition useful to investors, funders and partners without presenting strategic intent as completed validation.

  1. 01
    Strategic relevance

    Define the decision, buyer need and leverage created by the shared evidence core.

    Stop if the problem or accountable decision-maker is unclear.
  2. 02
    Public evidence

    Connect the proposition to attributable company, technical and research sources.

    Stop if a material statement cannot be traced to its source.
  3. 03
    Next validation

    Name the bounded test, independent authority and measurable outcome required next.

    Stop while the method or decision authority is unresolved.
  4. 04
    Qualified diligence

    Use a scoped review to assess the supporting case and agree the proof milestone.

    Keep public orientation distinct from a formal external decision.
Partner-fit architecture

Choose the missing authority, not the most impressive logo.

Aureon is founder-led and at an early stage. The useful partner is the organisation that can own a specific gap, test or route—not a decorative association.

Technical challenge

Independent architecture or security reviewer

Challenge assumptions, controls, threat boundaries and evidence quality against a written scope.

Required record: method, findings, limitations and corrective actions.
Research authority

Eligible research lead or laboratory

Own an appropriate test programme, reproducibility route or funder-eligible research work package.

Required record: role, institutional approval, protocol and decision rights.
Infrastructure

Compute or deployment environment

Provide bounded resources under explicit access, security, cost, data and evaluation conditions.

Required record: allocation, terms, measured run and retained evidence.
Mission-aligned capital

Investor with relevant diligence capability

Assess company, market, technical risk, capital fit and next validation milestone without importing unsupported traction.

Required record: decision, conditions and agreed authority.
