Research is the proving ground

The question creates interest. The method has to survive the test.

Ambitious questions pressure-test the same discipline used by every blade: sources, assumptions, null models, review gates and correction paths remain inspectable. The public research makes the method inspectable; independent review remains open.

Method spotlight · 24 July 2026

The protocol that replaced a presumed positive outcome with a pre-registered null.

Version 1 presupposed a positive energy result. The public Version 2 record replaces that assumption with a pre-registered hypothesis and null, an explicit decision rule, controls, dual instrument chains and raw-data archiving.

The record states that neither energy extraction nor the desired output has yet been demonstrated. That correction—visible, versioned and source-linked—is the research standard Aureon is building its programme around.

The operating mechanism

Evidence infrastructure for decisions that cannot afford ambiguity.

From research question to accountable decision, one trace carries purpose, provenance, evidence state, human authority and the final receipt forward. Every stop remains visible.

  1. 01

    Question

    Define the decision, accountable decision-maker and condition that must stop the workflow.

  2. 02

    Source

    Record origin, freshness, permission and the part of the source actually used.

  3. 03

    Claim

    Separate observed fact, interpretation, company record and unresolved gap.

  4. 04

    Human gate

    An authorised reviewer accepts, rejects or returns the record before action.

  5. 05

    Decision

    Retain the result, evidence basis, authority and any condition still open.

Current product and operating model. This is not a claim that every workflow is deployed, certified or independently validated.
