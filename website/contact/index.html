Make the first note useful

Four details are enough to start well.

Keep the first exchange concise, specific and connected to one investment or collaboration question.

Your context

What to include

  • Who: your organisation and relevant role.
  • Thesis: the Aureon opportunity most relevant to you.
  • Question: one specific point you want to explore.
  • Next step: the conversation or review you are proposing.

Subject pattern[route] — [organisation] — [specific request]

Good signal

Lead with substance

  • The market problem you believe is becoming urgent.
  • The platform capability that could create leverage.
  • The research or sector angle that differentiates Aureon.
  • The milestone that would change your conviction.

A thoughtful, focused note is more useful than a long generic introduction.
