Investor evidence language

Five labels make the opportunity easier to assess.

Each label tells an investor what can be inspected today and what must be demonstrated next.

Apply the key to the source register ↓

01

Source-linked

An original public record or artefact can be opened and checked at source.

Best for identity, attribution and persistent research records.
02

Company-built

Source, documentation or a working interface makes the Aureon implementation inspectable.

Best for reviewing architecture, workflow and product direction.
03

Research proposition

A defined method or model states a question that can be challenged and tested.

Best for judging originality, coherence and future option value.
04

Independently reviewed

A named external assessment has examined the stated method, system or outcome.

Best for increasing confidence through evidence outside Aureon.
05

Next validation

The specific experiment, review or design-partner milestone that can increase conviction.

Best for turning a broad vision into an assessable next step.
