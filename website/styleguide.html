Institutional design contract · Release 27

Editorial authority, without theatre.

Research creates the intellectual interest. Evidence state, source lineage and human review create the trust. The interface must keep those jobs distinct on every page.

Design principles

A research publisher first; an evidence instrument underneath.

The visual language borrows from annual reports, research institutes and technical journals: strong typography, documentary imagery, generous margins, rules rather than floating card walls, and a single clear action.

01

Lead with the question

Open with the hard problem, not a product slogan.

02

Show the source

Give every consequential statement a record, date and owner.

03

Mark the boundary

Separate publication, experiment, provider state and independent validation.

04

End in a decision

Make the human review gate and next action explicit.

Colour system

Warm paper, disciplined ink, sparse signals.

Ivory and white carry the reading experience. Ink provides structure. Gold orients; teal is reserved for verified state. Neither accent is decoration.

Evidence language

Five states; no silent promotion.

A stronger state requires new evidence. Publication volume, attractive artwork and company records cannot substitute for independent validation.

Source-linked

A named public source supports attribution or context.

--state-source

Company-recorded

An internal record exists under company control.

--state-company

Provider-confirmed

A named provider confirms the recorded state.

--state-provider

Independently validated

An independent body validates the relevant claim.

--state-independent

To verify

The proof gate is open, incomplete or unresolved.

--state-open
Typography

Source Serif for authority. Manrope for clarity. Plex Mono for records.

Headlines carry the cadence of a research journal. Interface copy stays direct. Dates, identifiers, DOIs, company numbers and evidence states use a technical register.

Difficult questions deserve inspectable tests.

Research imagery

Artwork can frame a question. It cannot upgrade the evidence.

Article artwork is paired only with its exact published note and links back to the source. Synthetic editorial images are labelled as concepts and must never imply owned facilities, completed experiments, engineering performance or validation.

Required: source URL, descriptive alt text, explicit editorial boundary and responsive image delivery.

Prohibited: generic laboratory imagery presented as operational proof, unsourced diagrams, decorative dashboards and speculative claims inside captions.

Layout and motion

A 4px base, documentary rules and purposeful transitions.

Spacing runs from 4px to 96px. Radius and shadow are restrained. Motion is limited to short opacity and transform transitions that explain hierarchy or state; there is no parallax, scroll-jacking, marquee or ambient loop.

--space-1: 4px · --space-24: 96px

--radius-1 → --radius-4

--motion-fast: 120ms · --motion-slow: 320ms

Reduced-motion mode removes non-essential transitions.

Accessibility contract

The argument remains usable in every mode.

Every page keeps one H1, landmarks, a skip link, visible keyboard focus, meaningful alt text and a coherent reading order. JavaScript enhances disclosure and filtering but does not hide the core evidence.

Required widths: 360, 768, 1024, 1440 and 1920 CSS pixels.

Navigation is keyboard-operable, closes with Escape and remains available without JavaScript.

Text and state colours must meet WCAG AA against their actual rendered backgrounds.
