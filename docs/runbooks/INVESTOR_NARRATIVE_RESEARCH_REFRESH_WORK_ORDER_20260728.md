## Status and authority

This is a design-and-evidence brief for a **future, separately authorised**
candidate. It does not start a successor to V40–V44; alter canonical website
files; create a package; access Home.pl; or authorise deployment.

V44 closed the prior candidate cycle on performance, while V43 established
that deferred rendering must not change document geometry. Any future
candidate needs a new exact-path work order, source-bound initial gate,
repeatability evidence, named human visual review and the normal owner
release gate.

## The investor story to clarify

The company should read as a serious, research-led systems business with one
reusable evidence core—not as a generic AI product and not as an unstructured
collection of unrelated ideas.

The proposed reading sequence is:

`decision problem → Aureon OS product loop → first commercial wedge →
inspectable public evidence → research-to-option discipline → founder
conversation`

The internal “Swiss Army knife” idea should be expressed as *one reusable
core, specialised evidence-gated applications*. Do not use the phrase itself
in investor-facing copy: it can imply lack of focus rather than platform
leverage.

## Visual and motion contract

- Take the discipline of Benchling’s research legibility, Vanta’s restrained
  trust posture and Datasite’s lifecycle clarity—not their claims, imagery,
  metrics, logos or product terminology.
- Existing Substack illustrations may be reused only within their established
  provenance and editorial-description boundary. Do not introduce unverified
  artwork or portray an illustration as a facility, experiment or product.
- Use sparse editorial layout, measured whitespace, one strong source/evidence
  visual and high-quality typographic hierarchy.
- Motion is optional: opacity/transform only, brief, non-blocking and fully
  disabled or reduced under `prefers-reduced-motion`. No autoplay video,
  marquee, scroll-jacking or layout-affecting reveal behaviour.
- Do not add a generic card dashboard, a fabricated interface, animated
  statistics or a “secure/continuous/agentic/data-room” claim.

## Required validation before a human visual decision

1. Record the source files, expected changed paths and candidate tree hash.
2. Run the website audit and require no broken local links, metadata,
   accessibility, schema, claim-boundary or source-consistency regressions.
3. Run the source-bound candidate initial gate, then the fixed sequential
   performance series only if it is eligible. Keep the existing 300 ms
   long-task budget; do not retry-shop a passing outlier.
4. Run the full visual matrix, including the deferred-render geometry gate,
   keyboard and reduced-motion checks.
5. Require named human review of desktop and mobile screenshots for the
   homepage, investor brief, projects, diligence and research routes.
6. Only after all gates pass may an owner consider canonical promotion,
   package verification, a fresh Home.pl backup and live HTTPS read-back.
