# Aureon Website Competitor and Position Design Brief

**Audit date:** 21 August 2026

**Owner:** Competitor / Position Audit

**Decision scope:** current official competitor home pages compared with the local V45 source in `website/`
**Release boundary:** this brief assesses the V45 candidate. It does not establish that V45 is live on Home.pl.

## Executive decision

V45 materially closes the earlier **toy-company** and **one-trick-pony** problems.

- The toy-company problem is closed at the candidate-design level. V45 uses an editorial, instrument-grade visual system; a real system map; restrained, functional motion; conservative evidence language; mature typography; and direct routes to company, research and implementation records. The primary pages no longer depend on novelty character art or generic AI spectacle.
- The one-trick-pony problem is closed at the proposition and information-architecture level. The site now explains one common thesis across Harmonic Nexus Core, Aureon OS and Evidence OS, then separates commercial evidence operations, mission-specific assurance and gated physical-system research by maturity and next proof.
- The remaining positioning risk is not narrowness but **portfolio sprawl**. In the home-page map, commercial decision workflows and unvalidated physical-system routes receive similar visual weight. The maturity labels are accurate, but an investor can still initially read the company as a laboratory catalogue before reading it as a focused commercial platform with research option value.

No observed competitor pattern requires a V45 redesign before a controlled release. V45 is a defensible serious baseline if its package, Home.pl deployment and live read-back all pass. The next design cycle should make the commercial wedge and primary conversion action more dominant without hiding the research programme.

## Method and limits

This is a dated, primary-source-only review. The observations below came from the rendered desktop home page and visible navigation of each company's official website on 21 August 2026. Asset and motion observations are limited to that rendered state; responsive variants, experiments and personalisation can differ. The review does not infer company performance, financial strength or product quality from website design.

No competitor wording, artwork, layout code or proprietary visual expression is licensed for copying. The only permitted output is an Aureon-specific inference about hierarchy, proof, interaction and conversion.

The comparison set deliberately spans:

- evidence, provenance and decision platforms;
- serious AI and research organisations;
- multi-sector deep-tech companies;
- an enterprise product site with high-spec motion and outcome proof.

## Detailed observations and Aureon implications

### Palantir

**Factual home-page observation.** The UK route opens with a region-specific outcome proposition over a dark technical video. Its visible page structure moves through trusted critical organisations, news and impact, data security and privacy, careers, current writing and a demo route. The navigation exposes products, impact studies, documentation, company, newsroom and investor relations.

**What Aureon should adapt.** Keep Belfast, the legal operator and the specific operating context visible. State the outcome of the platform before explaining all internal nouns. Treat privacy, authority and evidence controls as product substance rather than compliance footer copy.

**What Aureon should avoid.** Do not borrow Palantir's militarised, flag-led or monolithic institutional aesthetic. Aureon's differentiation is inspectable claim discipline and human authority, not an imitation of national-security scale.

### Truepic

**Factual home-page observation.** The hero changes the named visual-risk workflow while keeping one stable product explanation and two stable actions. Product imagery shows the capture interface and the kinds of authenticity/risk signals a reviewer would see. The page then follows a clean pain → operation → benefit → use-case → measured-result sequence.

**What Aureon should adapt.** Show one bounded review packet sooner. The V45 projects route already demonstrates how an ambiguous ORCID claim becomes a source-bound release decision; that concrete product experience should sit closer to the investor entry point. Translate the same mechanism into named buyer contexts without suggesting one universal implementation.

**What Aureon should avoid.** Do not create fake product screens, customer situations or rotating claims. Any buyer-context switch must preserve a static, accessible reading path and display only routes Aureon can truthfully scope.

### Chainalysis

**Factual home-page observation.** The company explains itself as one intelligence layer, immediately names four buyer problems, and uses a single demo action. Seven canvas elements support an animated network field in the inspected render. Customer logos and dated scale metrics appear before detailed products; differentiated evidence and customer stories come before the industry grid.

**What Aureon should adapt.** Keep the one-layer/many-applications logic, but give the buyer problem one screen of its own. Use a proof ladder: public identity and implementation first, bounded external route signals second, independent technical and customer outcome gates explicitly open.

**What Aureon should avoid.** Do not imitate a logo wall or quantitative outcomes without public permission and direct evidence. Aureon's current provider metrics must remain labelled as attention/activity, not customers, adoption or scientific validation.

### Anthropic

**Factual home-page observation.** Research and products are paired in the opening proposition while remaining separate destinations. The rendered page is editorial and restrained: a warm neutral background, oversized typography, abundant space, no video or canvas in the inspected state, and lightweight transitions. Policy, commitments, transparency and trust are part of the main company story.

**What Aureon should adapt.** Preserve the current separation of research proposition, company-built implementation and commercial evaluation. The V45 research route already applies this discipline well. Use visual restraint as a credibility signal; not every section needs ambient motion.

**What Aureon should avoid.** Do not copy Anthropic's sparse institutional expression or make values substitute for a buyer proposition. Aureon is earlier and must carry more direct source evidence on the page.

### SandboxAQ

**Factual home-page observation.** The company differentiates a named modelling approach from generic LLMs, explains the architecture visually, then applies it to multiple scientific markets. Publications, current news, attributed investor statements and enterprise logos support the portfolio story. The inspected render uses a black/gold technical identity and two video elements.

**What Aureon should adapt.** Keep HNC as the common research proposition and explicitly show how Aureon OS and Evidence OS convert that proposition into repeatable software controls. Group the portfolio under a small number of value lanes, as V45 now does, instead of presenting an undifferentiated invention catalogue.

**What Aureon should avoid.** Do not imitate award-led hero hierarchy, investor endorsement language or large funding figures. Aureon's approved route signals are next-step evidence, not grants, partnerships, customers or investment.

### Quantinuum

**Factual home-page observation.** The opening performance statement includes a visible qualification marker. The page then establishes mission, separates quantum computers, cloud/developer tooling and domain software, and only then shows customer projects and sector relevance. The inspected render uses three video elements, named technical photography and a separate investor-relations route.

**What Aureon should adapt.** Keep claim-state qualifiers next to the ambitious statement, not several sections later. Present core, control plane and applications as a stack. Give each physical-system proposition a direct next measurement or independent-review gate.

**What Aureon should avoid.** Do not lead with superlatives such as best, first or most accurate without an authoritative comparison and visible citation. Do not use laboratory imagery to imply facilities, hardware or test results Aureon does not possess.

### Darktrace

**Factual home-page observation.** The page opens with one urgent buyer problem and a product action, then progresses to the platform, third-party recognition, named customer outcomes, technical method and current resources. The rendered hero uses network video and a high-contrast purple/orange palette; one video and extensive SVG use were present in the inspected state.

**What Aureon should adapt.** Make the buyer's consequence as clear as the mechanism. When verified customer or evaluation outcomes exist, present each as a named, source-bound case with a precise time window and boundary.

**What Aureon should avoid.** Do not adopt generic cyber-neon styling, moving network theatre or analyst-leader language. Aureon's motion should continue to explain evidence flow, review state or system relationship.

## V45 source assessment

### Information architecture

The primary navigation—Core, Applications, Research, Evidence, Company, Investor brief—is materially stronger than a conventional Projects/About menu. It explains the company model without requiring the visitor to know Aureon's internal vocabulary.

The home page creates a coherent sequence across:

1. common core and system map;
2. public footprint;
3. maturity-labelled application lanes;
4. research questions and source routes;
5. evidence/authority method;
6. legal, diligence and founder conversion.

The structure is credible, but the buyer pain arrives after architecture and portfolio breadth. The strongest comparison sites state the consequential buyer problem in or immediately after the hero.

### Visual system

The local candidate render uses a cream institutional header over a dark teal technical field, a strong editorial serif for major statements, a disciplined sans-serif for operations, gold as a proof/authority accent and cyan as a system/data accent. The home system map is code-native and communicates actual relationships: HNC at the centre, Aureon OS and Evidence OS as distinct layers, and four application branches with maturity language.

This is distinctive without depending on competitor art, stock 3D or a fake dashboard. It reads as a research systems company rather than a toy or themed AI project.

### Motion and interaction

The home map has one slow 34-second orbit. Major instrument panels use a short 720 ms entrance, interactive packet/research states use short panel changes, and the progressive reveal fails open when `IntersectionObserver` is absent. Reduced-motion media rules stop the orbit, entrances and state animations.

This is more restrained than the video-heavy deep-tech set and closer to Anthropic's credibility-through-restraint. It also satisfies Aureon's constitution: the motion explains a system relationship or state change instead of creating continuous spectacle.

### Proof and research credibility

V45 has unusually strong claim-state discipline for an early company site:

- legal identity is directly linked;
- ORCID is named as a public work-group index, not peer review;
- GitHub activity is separated from users and adoption;
- Academia.edu figures are labelled as provider-reported attention;
- Edinburgh, EPCC, InterTradeIreland and Invest NI signals are explicitly bounded as route progress rather than grants, partnerships, customers, awards or investment;
- research illustrations are captioned as editorial rather than experimental evidence;
- HNC and physical-system routes retain open independent-review and measurement gates.

This is a real differentiator. It should remain visible, but boundary copy should not dominate every screen so heavily that the product's positive value disappears.

### Buyer pain and commercial wedge

The investor page is clearer than the home page: it names evidence-heavy R&D and regulated teams, scattered sources, technical claims, human approvals and reviewable decisions. It also provides a sensible staged route from bounded workflow to reusable delivery and platform scale.

The residual issue is hierarchy. Three hero actions have similar weight; the commercial evidence route, sector-control route and gated research options can still feel co-equal. The site should visually make **scoped evidence operations now** the primary entry, **domain control packs** the repeatable expansion, and **physical-system research** the gated option value.

### Investor readiness

V45 is investor-readable because it exposes the legal operator, thesis, software, research trail, public activity, route signals, maturity states, open gates and founder action. It correctly avoids internal company figures and unsupported commercial claims.

It is not an investor-relations site and should not pretend to be one. At this stage, the correct conversion is a qualified founder conversation or bounded evaluation packet backed by public diligence—not an invented traction dashboard.

## Priority design actions for the next cycle

### Release-baseline actions

These are refinements, not blockers to a controlled V45 release with live read-back.

1. **Make one commercial action primary.** Standardise the highest-intent CTA to either “Talk to the founder” or “Define an evaluation packet.” Keep “Inspect the evidence” as the consistent secondary route. Reduce the number of equally weighted hero buttons.
2. **Bring buyer consequence forward.** On the home page, place a compact buyer-problem/outcome band directly after the public proof strip, before the broader research/editorial sequence.
3. **Rebalance the hero map.** Preserve all branches but give Decision workflows / scoped evaluation the strongest visual state, Materials & signals the next state, and unmeasured physical-system branches a quieter outline treatment. This makes maturity visible before the labels are read.
4. **Surface the concrete packet.** Link or preview the public ORCID claim-decision receipt from the investor page before the full portfolio. This is Aureon's equivalent of a product screenshot, without inventing a customer dashboard.
5. **Keep motion scarce.** Retain the slow system orbit and state transitions. Add no autoplay background video, scroll hijack, continuous particle field or generic cosmic animation unless a measured relationship cannot be explained more clearly without it.

### Evidence-growth actions

1. Convert the first authorised evaluation into a source-bound case record only after outcome wording, customer permission and evidence are available.
2. Add external technical-review artefacts beside the relevant branch, not as a generic company badge.
3. Keep attention, engineering activity, route progress, validation and adoption as separate proof classes.
4. Maintain a dated public snapshot and machine-readable source for every visible metric.

## Emulate, adapt and avoid

### Emulate as a principle

- one stable core mechanism;
- immediate buyer consequence;
- a small, repeatable CTA model;
- proof placed near the claim it supports;
- named research and trust routes in primary navigation;
- product or workflow demonstration before the full sector catalogue;
- strong visual restraint where evidence is still emerging.

### Adapt specifically for Aureon

- the HNC → Aureon OS → Evidence OS stack;
- maturity-labelled sector blades;
- a review packet as the main product artefact;
- public research questions as option value, not product readiness;
- Belfast, NI696693, the legal operator and founder authority as trust infrastructure;
- motion as evidence assembly, route sealing and review-state change.

### Avoid

- competitor colour systems, illustrations, layouts or wording;
- customer/logo walls without permission;
- unqualified superlatives;
- funding, adoption, revenue or customer figures without a current public source;
- treating correspondence or meetings as partnerships or grants;
- speculative hardware imagery that implies a facility or prototype;
- generic SaaS blobs, cyber-neon, stock 3D and fake dashboards;
- animation whose only purpose is to make the page feel busy.

## Final position statement

V45 should present Aureon as **one evidence-native research-to-decision architecture with a focused commercial entry and a disciplined portfolio of separately gated applications**.

That is stronger than “an AI startup,” more coherent than “a collection of inventions,” and more truthful than claiming every research route is already a product. The serious-company signal comes from the combination of architecture, legal identity, public implementation, research provenance, explicit evidence boundaries and a concrete next evaluation—not from visual theatre alone.
