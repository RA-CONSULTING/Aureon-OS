12 September 2026. The broad experiment-repair goal remains active.

**Pilot 6502006 completed with exit 0. All 7,680 logical responses were downloaded and audited.** The allocation lasted 13 minutes 13 seconds on one node with four NVIDIA GH200 GPUs. It used approximately 0.220278 node-hours and 0.881111 GPU-hours, calculated from the scheduler receipt. These are allocation estimates, not a refreshed portal debit.

## What was repaired

The new benchmark gives the action policy as an explicit, controlled factor and distinguishes a refuted factual claim from missing evidence. Trusted metadata supplies permissions and approved prior constraints; source documents cannot grant authority or issue instructions. Every condition receives the same evidence. The exact twelve repository-generated context strings and pinned model adapter remain fixed.

The generator supplies 5,120 exact distinct inputs: 1,280 development cases and 3,840 evaluation cases. There are 80 declared templates, with 60 held out for evaluation. Numeric and identifier changes are not new independent tasks: after normalizing those changes, 113 input forms remain, and several templates share mechanisms. Results therefore use paired template summaries, equal family weighting, family sensitivity checks and explicit limits on independence.

Every HTTP attempt now has a synced request and response receipt. Completed responses survive restart; invalid answers remain failures and are not retried for a better score. Strict JSON validation rejects duplicate keys, invented citations, malformed confidence, extra text and truncated output. Sixteen fixture/journal checks passed locally and remotely, and a seventeenth remote check exercised the real pinned adapter against an inert endpoint, including a transport retry, malformed answer and restart. Four separate analysis checks passed locally.

These are synthetic prompt-context experiments. The memory condition removes a prompt reminder, not a working memory store; the inherited role condition changes text, not an implemented routing network. This does not test the entire live Aureon system or establish physical/scientific claims.

## Measured pilot throughput

The pilot used 80 development cases across 24 context/policy arms, repeated in four blocks on one persistent server. Its 7,680 calls are throughput evidence, not additional held-out cases.

| Concurrent requests | Completed calls across two blocks | Measured calls/second | Terminal transport failures |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 32 | 3,840 | 10.233 | 0 |
| 128 | 3,840 | 19.312 | 0 |

The frozen rule selected concurrency **128**. No out-of-memory or preemption warning matched the audit log check. **1 malformed model answer** remains a scored failure; it was not retried. There were no unresolved HTTP attempts. Per-block timings, raw outcomes and GPU samples are retained in the audit. Startup and shutdown are included in the allocation receipt but excluded from the reported request-service rates.

The one-second GPU samples were recovered for all four devices with no unparsed rows. Mean sampled utilization during the two 32-request blocks was 85.7% and 93.2%; during the two 128-request blocks it was 68.5% and 81.1%. Higher request throughput is the expansion criterion. GPU busy percentage alone does not measure useful answers per unit of allocation. The initial audit summary omitted telemetry because its display-unit parser did not strip the percent sign; that summary is preserved, and the corrected audit rechecks the same immutable raw files.
