# Isambard experiment results

Updated 12 September 2026. **There are reproducible numerical/software results, a limited QGITA benchmark improvement, and several substantial negative findings.** The new complete retrospective Stage 1 comparison shows that the full Aureon condition underperformed its baseline. The HNC follow-up supports the beta=1 reference under its tested finite-time rule. The evidence does not establish broad Aureon superiority or the proposed physical interpretations.

**HNC:** production 6498143 completed on 32 nodes / 128 GPUs in 1h57m30s. All 128 cells reached 16,777,216 steps; artifact audit 6498144 passed across 2,176 receipts and 4,352 result/checkpoint hashes. All 15,488 tested beta=1 cases meet the finite-time rule. The median transition moved to 1.000015067, but late escapes continue. See `HNC-fine-grid-results-2026-09-12.md`.

**Full-system Stage 1:** recovery 6500368 supplied the missing 960-response shard. Its unchanged frozen benchmark still failed on five invalid JSON replies. Retrospective audit 6500947 nevertheless verified the complete 122,880-response matrix under a declared amendment that retains all invalid model replies at their original zero score. Full verdict accuracy is 65.98% versus 67.70% baseline, and the composite score is also lower. Both exploratory 95% difference intervals favour the baseline under the inherited analysis. **Design limits:** 5,120 fixture records contain only 514 distinct user prompts, and the action-selection policy is underspecified. The intervals are not generalization intervals for thousands of independent tasks. See `Stage1-retrospective-results-2026-09-12.md` and `Stage1-error-diagnosis-2026-09-12.md`.

**Acoustics:** normalization, frequency filtering, residual checks and the Schumann comparison are repaired. The consolidated amended assessment supports generic closed-room resonances; distinctiveness and corrected Schumann gates fail. The simplified opening model also fails the target and octave robustness gates. The original frozen verdict remains inconclusive. See `Acoustics-amended-assessment-2026-09-12.md`.

**QGITA:** the supplemental audit passed exact decision-ledger equality and structural/statistical checks on Isambard. It preserves the original byte-identity failure and all 3,386 diagnostic differences. Missing-data performance remains poor.

**Original papers:** the GitHub history index covers 3,431 reachable commits, 36,396 file-content versions and 180 distinct native documents. The public-source layer covers 75 Academia papers and two talks, the source sections of 29 Substack essays, and a checksum-verified Master Equations PDF reached through ResearchGate and Zenodo. Its record dates to 5 November 2025 and still contains mixed beta wording. The oldest recovered compilation has July 2025 creation metadata, which does not independently date each passage. The original reprint chronology and complete file-level review of every public upload remain unresolved. See `Public-original-paper-trail-2026-09-12.md` and `GitHub-research-history-2026-09-12.md`.

**Access and compute:** terminal access and browser sign-in are verified, although new SSH connections have been intermittent. The refreshed portal reports 86.9 hours of all-time usage and an estimated monthly closing balance of 4,913.09 NHR. Access ends 22 November 2026. A conditional multi-family budget and earlier verification/export cutoffs are recorded in `Isambard-compute-plan-2026-09-12.md`; these are planning ceilings, not already submitted experiments.

## Other experiments and engineering checks

- **Repository coverage:** the 670-file sweep recorded **559 passed, 15 failed, 12 interrupted, 1 timeout and 83 with no tests**. These are file outcomes, not individual test counts. All 3,862 Python files in the module sweep compiled; of 1,138 modules, 1,073 imported, 42 had dependency/import errors, 21 runtime errors and 2 timed out. Across 44 branches, 37 compiled but only 6 installed successfully. This is substantial coverage with unresolved failures, not a clean whole-repository pass.
- **Targeted remediation:** the later 212-item test session completed successfully, including one skip, on a modified worktree. It demonstrates the targeted repairs, not that every earlier failure was eliminated. The daemon sandbox recorded 9 activated, 13 errors and 14 bounded timeouts across 36 daemons.
- **Custody and replay:** one custody audit verified **3,519/3,519 records**. The sealed QGITA package passed 42 tests and reproduced three JSON results semantically. The UCR audit separately passed its byte-identity checks; the same overall job later failed robustness byte-identity verification. These stages must remain separate.
- **QGITA NAB and robustness:** five eligible NAB series contained 12 labelled windows; the detector issued 7 alerts, with 2 inside windows and 5 outside (precision 28.6%, window recall 16.7%). Missing-data robustness scores deteriorated. The deposited robustness reproduction differed from its reference, so it cannot support a verified robustness claim.
- **HNC large sweeps:** reviewed receipts cover 134,217,728 FP32 trajectories, 16,777,216 FP64 trajectories and a 2,493,568-trajectory grand matrix. Their run lengths differ, so their escape fractions cannot isolate a precision effect. The β-edge follow-up found 1,710 of 8,367 trajectories bounded at 65,536 steps escaped by 262,144 steps in its canonical horizon leg.
- **HNC V3 pilot:** job 6368229 passed **114/114 tests across five files**, repeated D1–D3 self-tests deterministically, and passed **4/4 GPU parity checks**. The D4 command-line lane returned `no_data` because it required a fresh receipt; the main array remained **HOLD**. The pilot does not complete the broader V3 campaign.
- **Gamma broadcast:** a common value reached ten gates with zero observed spread and strong synchronized tightening. One of eight self-checks failed because of clipping. An isolated probe accepted a synthetic envelope asserting the expected source: asserted source identity was not authenticated provenance. Missing Gamma left local limits unchanged.
- **Conscience veto:** the direct 0.20 threshold behaved correctly. However, none of 14,010 sampled generated protocols approached that threshold (minimum 0.3597). Replacing φ with √2 changed scores but produced zero veto flips in 10,000 paired samples. The mechanism exists; useful activation and unique φ dependence were not demonstrated by these samples.
- **King's weights:** changing weights altered synthetic consensus/actions, particularly when inverted. Replacing φ with √2 caused only 4 consensus and 5 action flips in 366 cases. Weight sensitivity does not establish that φ is uniquely necessary. No automatic actions occurred in the tested cases and probes.
- **MCP operator:** four prohibited/injection probes were refused before dispatch and ten envelope seals verified. Five allowed calls crossed the boundary, although one returned a missing-manifest error. This was an offline loopback test with encryption disabled and wire sequence numbers stuck at zero; replay protection was only separately library-tested. It is a bounded integration result, not security certification.
- **φ clustering:** the deposited catalogue analysis did not establish a special φ pattern; φ lost to alternative spacing controls, and its adjusted p-value was 1.0.
- **Math Angel:** the reported “Z≈68” was a fixed output string, not a computed empirical statistic. A model-consistent simulation instead reported Z≈3.80. The original unseeded trajectory cannot be exactly recovered, and forced alignment is part of the model. The original 68-sigma interpretation is unsupported.
- **L(t):** the reported 12.85 and 13.03 scores were sums of fixed inputs, not measured time-series significance. The corresponding null was a point mass that exceeded the quoted thresholds every time. Those thresholds provide no significance calibration for this implementation.
- **Observational claims:** the deposited September 6 audit found missing activation/traffic records prevented several correlation and timing claims from being recomputed. Market percentages depended on the chosen interval and endpoint convention; astronomical angular proximity did not supply a search-corrected significance result. Predictions labelled pending in that audit have not been rescored here as current events.
- **Schumann/intent:** the September 6 audit found **0/35 required station-days** available in its searched claim window. The claimed effect therefore had no computable effect size or p-value. Tests on other available images validate parts of the analysis pipeline, not the missing claim-window observation.
- **EPAS:** six self-checks passed, but the subsequent audit stopped on a missing dependency. No completed E1 physical result was established in the reviewed records. Other registry entries without deposited result receipts remain unverified.

## What to carry forward

The strongest result to develop is **QGITA's narrowly defined UCR localization improvement**, with its strict-metric and robustness limitations explicit. HNC has a reproducible numerical basis and a measured boundary correction worth incorporating. Tandem has a substantial detector improvement but still fails its own target. The complete retrospective Stage 1 comparison now shows lower full-system performance; prospective work should follow diagnosis and repair, not a superiority claim.

The negative results are actionable: revise the HNC boundary, reconsider the LiDAR feature design, resolve workforce execution failures, and repair statistical calibration before reusing the Math Angel or L(t) significance language. Existing software tests and synthetic outcomes do not supply independent evidence of physical fields, consciousness, retrocausality or excess energy.

Corrections here include the newer Tandem learned-detector result (56.62%, rather than its stale 24.12%), the failed strict acoustics solver gate, and the distinction between the passed UCR audit and failed robustness verification. Successful scheduler completion is not itself experimental success; arrays, retries and overlapping profiles are not independent experiments.
