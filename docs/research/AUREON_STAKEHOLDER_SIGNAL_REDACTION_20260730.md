# Aureon stakeholder design-signal redaction

Recorded: 30 July 2026  
Purpose: privacy-safe design input for the investor website information
architecture.

## Authority and privacy boundary

This record is a human-created redacted abstraction of current stakeholder
questions. The original correspondence remains with its provider and is not
copied into the repository.

This record deliberately contains no sender, recipient, organisation,
subject, quotation, email address, message or thread identifier, attachment
name, provider URL, meeting link, private financial term or unpublished
company figure.

The questions establish areas that a reader wants clarified. They do not
establish investment interest, endorsement, market validation, customer use,
funding, partnership, scientific validation or commercial traction.

## Required response closure

A design worker may receive only the stable signal identifier, controlled
topic and observation codes, route scope, existing public claim identifiers,
permitted response code and a deterministic capsule hash.

For every signal marked for public response, a staged candidate must record:

1. the signal identifier;
2. the chosen disposition and response code;
3. the single route being addressed;
4. changed paths within that route's existing allow-list;
5. existing public claim identifiers used; and
6. the exact stakeholder capsule hash.

The response manifest proves scope and disposition only. It does not prove
that the design improved, create public wording permission, or bypass claim,
accessibility, visual, performance, owner or release review.
