# ORCID public record reconciliation — 30 July 2026

## Scope and decision

This is a read-only reconciliation of the official public ORCID works record
with the canonical website research route. It does not amend the website,
authorise a release, or establish peer review, independent validation,
product readiness, adoption, funding, partnership or endorsement.

**Decision:** keep the website's curated research orientation and retain the
public ORCID profile as the route to the complete body of work. A bulk card
expansion would weaken the investor reading path and blur different research
record types.

## Website representation

`website/data/research-catalogue.json` deliberately presents six orientation
records rather than mirroring every ORCID group. This remains the correct
information architecture:

1. a reader receives a bounded cross-section of the research;
2. every featured record retains a direct persistent source route;
3. the ORCID profile supplies the complete public index; and
4. research availability remains separate from validation or product claims.

## Public-language boundary

Safe public wording is:

> Public source-linked research record; the link establishes attribution and
> availability. Independent review, validation and application evidence remain
> separate.

Do not convert a work count, title, DOI, publication date, download, profile
or editorial note into evidence of scientific validation, operational
performance, customer use, investment, partnership, grant success or market
adoption.

## Future refresh rule

Any new featured research card must use the exact source title, record type,
publication date, persistent identifier and public URL; carry its evidence
boundary beside the link; and pass the existing candidate, claim,
accessibility, visual, performance and owner-release controls. The direct
ORCID route remains sufficient until a specific source-bound addition is
approved.
