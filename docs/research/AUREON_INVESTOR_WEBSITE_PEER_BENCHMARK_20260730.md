# Aureon investor website peer benchmark

Checked: 30 July 2026  
Scope: first-party public websites; category framing, platform breadth,
evidence hierarchy, visual restraint and purposeful motion.

## Authority and research boundary

This is a design-pattern record, not a comparative performance claim or an
endorsement. It records high-level observations from official public company
sites. Aureon must not copy competitor copy, source code, imagery, diagrams,
screenshots, CSS, interaction sequences, visual identity or trade dress.

## Cross-market conclusions

The strongest sites consistently:

1. lead with one category-defining sentence;
2. present breadth as branches from a shared platform or operating core;
3. use research to support the commercial proposition rather than replace it;
4. use motion to demonstrate a system, data flow, physical asset or state
   transition;
5. put each claim beside an inspectable product, publication, contract,
   customer record or explicit status; and
6. create seriousness through typography, spacing and evidence hierarchy
   rather than animation volume.

A long sector list before the reader understands the product is the recurring
pattern to avoid.

## Aureon design direction

The public site should show the company's "Swiss Army knife" quality without
using that phrase as its category claim:

1. **Commercial thesis** — evidence infrastructure for decisions that cannot
   afford ambiguity.
2. **Common core** — HNC research informs Aureon OS's governed source, claim,
   authority and receipt controls.
3. **Capability branches** — each route identifies its buyer or user, current
   evidence, limitation and next proof gate.
4. **Research loop** — Published → Implemented → Tested → Applied, with every
   transition labelled by its actual status.
5. **Evidence routes** — public research records, repositories, demonstrators,
   company record and diligence material remain separately inspectable.

Research breadth should appear as disciplined expansion after the first
commercial wedge, not as a claim that every route is already a product,
customer deployment, partnership or independently validated outcome.

## Visual and motion system

- Use an obsidian or midnight foundation, high-contrast typography and one
  restrained aurora-teal or warm metallic accent.
- Prefer an editorial grid, large type, measured whitespace, thin rules and
  precise evidence labels over decorative dashboard cards.
- Make the principal motion an original HNC evidence field or graph whose
  nodes resolve into research, governed software and bounded applications.
- Require a complete static state on first render. Motion must never delay the
  category statement, primary action or proof links.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` and provide an equivalent static diagram.
- Use no competitor media, screenshots, copied diagrams or unverified
  third-party artwork.

## Recommended homepage evidence sequence

1. Single commercial thesis and first-buyer pathway.
2. Verified proof rail using source links and status labels.
3. Original HNC-to-Aureon-OS architecture.
4. Published → Implemented → Tested → Applied research loop.
5. Sector applications using one evidence-and-boundary template.
6. Demonstrators and current Aureon OS capability.
7. Research and editorial explanation.
8. Evidence/status ledger.
9. Company and investor contact path.

This benchmark creates design direction only. A future candidate still
requires a bounded work order, source-bound claims, asset provenance,
accessibility and performance checks, visual QA, human acceptance, a verified
production backup and the separate WebsiteOperator release process.
