# Aureon editorial asset visual-risk review

Observed: 30 July 2026  
Scope: six responsive Substack editorial artwork pairs currently present in the local website tree  
Authority: visual and representational-risk review only; no copyright, release, package or deployment authority

## Decision

The artwork can add editorial interest to the public research notebook, but it
must not carry the investor proposition or appear to evidence facilities,
hardware, measurements, experiments or validated results.

The homepage and investor route should use a repo-native explanatory diagram of
the Evidence OS / HNC control flow instead. Article artwork should remain
secondary, permanently labelled and linked to its exact author-published note.

No asset is approved for public use by this review. Candidate eligibility still
requires a separate hash-bound provenance record, explicit human rights
decision and exact route/surface approval.

## Current-state finding

The local canonical website currently references editorial artwork on the
homepage, research route and research-notebook route. Several images are
photorealistic enough to be read as real apparatus, facilities or data even
when a nearby caption says otherwise.

Until an approved candidate is selected:

1. Do not add the artwork to any further route.
2. Do not use it on an investor, diligence, recognition or proof card.
3. Do not use artwork pixels as evidence for a public claim.
4. Do not hide the boundary in hover text, alt text alone or a transient
   animation.
5. Keep the global design-research artwork state `not-cleared`.

## Candidate acceptance conditions

- Every source and responsive variant is bound by exact SHA-256, byte count,
  detected MIME type and intrinsic dimensions.
- The public post URL, exact route, destination, alt text, caption and credit
  line are approved together.
- Higher-risk representation classes use the exact persistent boundary above
  or a stricter approved equivalent.
- The visual remains secondary to inspectable sources and claim-state labels.
- Browser QA proves the boundary remains visible at mobile and desktop
  breakpoints and with JavaScript or motion disabled.
- A separate code-native Evidence OS / HNC diagram carries the commercial
  explanation without using synthetic facility imagery.
