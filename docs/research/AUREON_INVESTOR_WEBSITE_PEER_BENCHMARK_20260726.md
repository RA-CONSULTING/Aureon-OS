# Aureon investor website peer benchmark

Checked: 26 July 2026  
Scope: live public website design, information architecture, proof hierarchy and investor conversion.

## Direction

Aureon should present one reusable evidence-and-control platform, a research
programme that creates differentiated option value, and sector applications
that inherit the same governance core. The public website should attract
qualified investors through inspectable public proof. Financing figures,
private route states and internal operating records remain in scoped
diligence.

## Observed peer patterns

| Peer | Observed pattern | Aureon application |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [SRI International](https://www.sri.com/) | Research divisions are separated from the sectors in which they create value; commercialisation has its own route. | Separate platform capabilities, research themes and sector applications so breadth reads as leverage. |
| [Anduril](https://www.anduril.com/) | A shared software spine sits beneath multiple domain products; cinematic media supports a simple category claim. | Show Evidence OS as the common spine and keep motion subordinate to comprehension. |
| [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/) | Research and governance are first-class destinations; transparency material is structurally prominent. | Keep evidence state, authority and validation boundaries visible without making the site defensive. |
| [Palantir](https://www.palantir.com/) | Named impact stories combine attributed sources, outcomes and supporting media. | Use evidence spotlights only where a named public source supports the exact claim. |
| [Planet](https://www.planet.com/) | Technical imagery carries source, scale and limitation captions; investor material is separated from product storytelling. | Caption concept art and technical visuals, and keep decision-sensitive diligence controlled. |
| [IBM Research](https://research.ibm.com/) | Research is connected to tangible tools, code and documentation. | Route papers to public source, methods, code or a clear next validation step. |
| [Google DeepMind](https://deepmind.google/) | Research discovery ladders from major questions to news and a searchable publication database. | Lead with flagship questions, then provide guided and searchable source routes. |
| [Rocket Lab](https://rocketlabcorp.com/) | One category claim is followed by an end-to-end capability explanation and operational proof. | State the category quickly, explain the shared core, then show sector relevance and proof. |

## Release criteria

1. The hero defines the category and investment thesis before enumerating sectors.
2. The architecture explains source, provenance, human authority and bounded output.
3. Sector cards show problem, shared capability, public evidence and next validation.
4. Research is organised around flagship questions and source routes, not catalogue counters.
5. ORCID, Zenodo, DOI, Companies House and public repository links remain directly inspectable.
6. Concept art, diagrams and editorial artwork carry provenance and limitation captions.
7. Motion is purposeful, reduced-motion safe and never required to understand primary copy.
8. Investor conversion is visible but dignified: investor brief, strategic fit and a direct conversation route.
9. Private financing, application, correspondence, provider and operator records do not enter the public release.
10. Legacy internal routes return 404 and the live release is verified by exact manifest read-back.
