## Core public message

**Aureon is evidence infrastructure for high-consequence decisions.**

The public story should use a coherent three-part structure:

1. **HNC** — company-authored research and systems-method work, presented as
   an evolving framework with explicit validation boundaries.
2. **Aureon OS** — the company-built evidence-control layer that keeps source
   lineage, claim state, human authority and retained decisions connected.
3. **Applied routes** — specialist workflows which must each earn their own
   buyer, domain evidence, qualified authority and measured result.

This communicates the "Swiss Army knife" ambition without presenting every
sector route as a deployed product, partnership, or independently validated
outcome.

## Visual and motion direction

The target is an institutional research-and-operations website: restrained,
high-signal, precise and reviewable. It should feel closer to a research
laboratory, technical assurance company or capital-markets data room than a
consumer AI landing page.

### Deck visual audit

The reviewed eight-slide investor deck provides a useful company-owned visual
baseline: black editorial typography, an off-white canvas, restrained orange
emphasis, thin rules, generous whitespace, and simple information groups. Its
best moves are the clear problem headline, the four-step product loop and the
staged commercial-path layout. These are principles to extend, not a website
template to copy verbatim.

For the website, strengthen the deck's calm system with source-linked research
visuals, a clearly bounded proof map and purposeful transition states. Avoid
turning the website into a slide deck or a grid of decorative UI cards. Do not
port the deck's internal operator counts (84 / 92 / 7 / 41) to the public
website: the deck itself labels them as ledger counts, and public copy has a
separate no-figures direction.

- Give the **problem → control loop → proof route** the visual hierarchy;
  research graphics should support that narrative rather than compete with it.
- Treat permitted Substack artwork as an editorial evidence layer: use it only
  with a known source/provenance and pair it with the linked research record.
  The current Gmail and Drive searches did not identify a definitive original
  artwork delivery thread, so no new external artwork is cleared by this
  brief.
- Use motion to reveal an evidence state, a handoff or a review gate. Respect
  reduced motion, never delay core reading, and keep a static complete state.
- Prefer sparse typography, strong grids, measured whitespace and purposeful
  diagrams over decorative dashboards, novelty effects or generic AI imagery.
