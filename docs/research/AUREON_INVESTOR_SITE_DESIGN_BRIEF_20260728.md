# Aureon investor website design brief — source-bound, no-figures

**Prepared:** 28 July 2026  
**Status:** planning specification only — not public copy, a release approval,
or a deployment instruction.

## Decision in one sentence

Lead with a narrowly understandable evidence-control wedge, then use HNC,
public research, Aureon OS and sector routes to show the depth and option value
behind it. The website must never ask an investor to infer the first buyer or
the product from a wide research portfolio.

## Authority and evidence boundary

This brief is deliberately non-authoritative. It does not establish revenue,
customers, production use, investment, grants, partnerships, independent
validation, scientific consensus, security certification or deployment. It
contains no financial figures, valuation, forecast, internal operating count,
or confidential investor material.

Every public change still needs a current claim-register entry, a staged
candidate work order, browser and performance evidence, named human visual
acceptance, owner-controlled source reconciliation, a verified backup and the
separate Home.pl release gate.

| Evidence input | What it can support | What it cannot support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `website/data/company-platform.json` | HNC as an evolving company-authored research and systems-method framework; Aureon OS as a company-built evidence operating system; mission blades as evidence-labelled applications. | An established new scientific field, product readiness, customer use or independent validation. |
| `website/data/operator-evidence.json` | An original architecture story: attributable source → provenance and claim state → accountable human authority → bounded output. | A claim that the architecture is operational in every sector or autonomously runs decisions. |
| `website/data/research-catalogue.json` and `artifacts/website-operator/AUREON_ORCID_RESEARCH_CATALOGUE_AUDIT_20260728T075108+0100.json` | An attributable ORCID research identity, a public work index and dated source availability. | Peer review, commercial performance or product validation. |
| `website/data/updates.json` and `data/website_operator/public_claim_evidence_register.v1.json` | Dated, bounded technical and research-attention signals when their source and date remain visible. | Users, customers, adoption, traction or independent endorsement. |
| `docs/research/AUREON_INVESTOR_NARRATIVE_ALIGNMENT_20260728.md` | The company-authored commercial framing and private-review feedback translated into a no-figures public narrative. | An investment decision or permission to publish confidential deck content. |
| `docs/research/AUREON_INVESTOR_WEBSITE_PEER_BENCHMARK_20260728.md` and the 28 July live peer scan | Abstract information-design patterns from serious research and deep-tech sites. | Comparative performance, copied expression, trade dress or a claim of market equivalence. |

The current public-claim register remains the wording authority. Proposed copy
in this brief is a design hypothesis until it has a source, boundary and
review expiry in that register.

## Narrative architecture

The public story has four layers. A reader should understand each before being
asked to inspect the next.

```text
1. Evidence-heavy decision problem
              ↓
2. Aureon OS evidence-control loop
              ↓
3. Public proof and research discipline
              ↓
4. Bounded sector pathways and the next proof gate
```

### 1. Commercial wedge: make the first reading path explicit

**Job to be done:** turn scattered source material into a reviewable,
human-accountable decision record without losing provenance, claim state or
decision context.

**First workflow to frame:** evidence operations for source-heavy research,
technical review, grant and diligence workflows. This is a proposed public
commercial framing, not a claim of existing customers or deployment.

**What should be visible above the fold:**

- The problem: evidence gets fragmented across documents, messages and tools;
  reviewers lose source lineage and accountability.
- The mechanism: keep what was used, excluded, interpreted, approved and
  handed off connected to the decision.
- The near-term proof: a bounded design-partner or independent review
  milestone, described as a next gate rather than an achieved result.

The approved company-positioning statements can support the first version of
this section:

> Aureon Zorza Technologies is a research-led systems and innovation company.
> Aureon OS is a company-built engineering and evidence operating system.

Any shorter headline such as “Evidence infrastructure for complex decisions”
is **proposed wording only**. It requires an owner-reviewed public-claim
record before it is placed on the site.

### 2. Product: show one calm operating model

Use an original, static-first evidence-control map based on the published
architecture rather than a generic AI dashboard:

| Stage | Plain-language role | Supporting public record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Attributable sources | Identify the material and its origin. | Public repositories, formal research records and clearly identified company material. |
| Provenance and claim state | Keep the evidence state and interpretation boundary attached. | `operator-evidence.json` architecture layer. |
| Accountable human authority | Preserve a named human decision gate and scope. | `operator-evidence.json` architecture layer. |
| Bounded output | Produce a reviewable record with a stated boundary and next proof. | `operator-evidence.json` architecture layer. |

The diagram must have a complete text equivalent, keyboard reading order and
static end state. If motion is used, it may reveal a handoff or a change of
evidence state; it must never be needed to understand the product.

### 3. Proof: make research a traceable evidence rail

Research should substantiate discipline, not substitute for a product story.
Place these elements after the operating model:

1. **Research identity** — ORCID as an attributable public work index.
2. **Formal records** — source-linked papers and Zenodo records, separated
   from author-published explanation.
3. **Inspectable implementation** — Aureon OS public source and tests as a
   technical inspection route, with no production-readiness inference.
4. **Dated attention signals** — only where the platform, date, definition and
   limitation are shown beside the signal.

Every item receives one compact evidence label chosen from the claim register:
`Company-authored`, `Research proposition`, `Public source available`,
`Public research record`, `Attention signal`, `Prospective application`, or
`External recognition`. The label must lead to a short “what this does not
prove” line, not a repeated wall of disclaimers.

### 4. Sector pathways: translate breadth into disciplined option value

The “Swiss Army knife” idea is credible only as one governed core with
separately earned applications. Show no more than three pathways in the main
reading path:

| Pathway | How to present it | Evidence condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence operations | The near-term commercial wedge for source-heavy review workflows. | Name the workflow and next proof; do not imply a customer deployment. |
| Technical and research assurance | A source-linked application area where provenance and human authority matter. | Link to the formal record and distinguish a research route from a product. |
| One selected physical-research option | Longer-horizon research option, not a general capability claim. | State maturity, limitation and the next external validation gate. |

All other blades belong in a clearly labelled research/archive route. Each
visible sector card must answer four questions in the same order: **decision or
buyer; current evidence state; exact limitation; next proof gate.**

## Information architecture for the next authorised candidate

The existing routes can carry this narrative without inventing a new sprawling
navigation system:

| Surface | Primary job | Required content order |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Homepage | Explain Aureon in under a minute. | Problem → evidence-control loop → evidence today → selected pathways → research depth. |
| Investor reading room | Answer diligence questions without publishing internal finance. | Commercial wedge → workflow → differentiation thesis → proof sources → current maturity and next proof. |
| Research | Let a technical reader inspect authorship and formal records. | Record type and source → research question → boundary → explanatory editorial material. |
| Projects | Show the core-to-application relationship. | HNC → Aureon OS → individual application → evidence state → next gate. |
| Diligence | Give a qualified reviewer an inspection route. | Company record → source routes → architecture boundary → controlled contact path. |

The navigation should prioritise **Platform**, **Research**, **Use cases** and
**Diligence**. “Funding” or “Investor” remains a reading room, not a homepage
of financial claims or internal operating records.

## Visual and motion direction

The visual target is an institutional research-and-operations surface:
confident but quiet, precise rather than theatrical, and legible before any
interaction.

- Use a sparse editorial grid, strong typographic hierarchy, generous
  whitespace and a small material palette. Research visuals should explain a
  source, relationship, state or proof route.
- Use original HNC/evidence-control diagrams and company-owned or
  source-cleared research imagery. Never use competitor visuals, screenshots,
  code, CSS, wording or trade dress as reference material to copy.
- Treat approved Substack artwork as an editorial bridge only when provenance,
  permission and the linked article record are present. Artwork must not imply
  a third-party validation or a product result.
- Reserve animation for a single meaningful system relationship: source →
  review → decision. Animate only `transform` and `opacity`, respect
  `prefers-reduced-motion`, give all motion an equivalent static explanation,
  lazy-load below-the-fold media with dimensions, and never defer hero copy or
  the primary call to action behind JavaScript or video.
- Do not use generic particle fields, cosmic decoration, rolling metric
  counters, customer-logo theatre, or a dashboard-shaped hero to compensate
  for an unclear commercial message.

## Copy and claim controls

| Use | Safe framing | Do not imply |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HNC | Evolving Aureon research and systems-method framework. | An accepted scientific field or independently validated universal theory. |
| Aureon OS | Company-built, inspectable engineering and evidence operating system. | Fully autonomous or production-ready operation. |
| Public code and tests | Technical inspection route for company-built mechanisms. | Customer use, operational safety or independent validation. |
| ORCID, Zenodo and papers | Attribution and availability of public research records. | Peer review or commercial/product readiness. |
| Platform analytics | Dated attention/access signals with methodology and limitations. | Traction, adoption, customers or revenue. |
| Sector pathways | Evidence-labelled application hypotheses with a next proof gate. | Partnerships, live deployments, outcomes or funding. |

Never publish the private deck’s funding figures, valuation, internal ledger
counts, commercial forecasts or contact routing. Never describe an investor
request for a deck as investment interest or an investment outcome.

## Completion criteria for a future candidate

1. Owner approves the local-versus-live reconciliation decision and a verified
   backup is present.
2. A fresh work order binds only the necessary pages, assets and copy changes
   to the current source hash and claim register.
3. Any proposed commercial headline receives its own bounded public-claim
   entry before it appears in a candidate.
4. The candidate passes source-bound claims checks, static/a11y checks,
   multi-browser visual QA, reduced-motion checks, geometry and long-task
   budgets.
5. A named human accepts the visual result after reviewing the intended
   desktop and mobile states.
6. The owner gate, package verification, Home.pl publish step and live
   manifest read-back independently succeed.

Until then, this brief is the controlled design target: **one legible core,
one first proof path, many carefully bounded applications.**
