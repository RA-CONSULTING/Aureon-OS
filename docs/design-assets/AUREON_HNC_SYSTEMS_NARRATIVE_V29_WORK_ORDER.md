# Aureon HNC systems narrative V29 work order

## Objective

Present Aureon Zorza Technologies as a research-led, multi-sector innovation
company without implying that authored research, public access signals, software
source, sector propositions, external discussion, or future ambition are
independent validation or commercial adoption.

The public hierarchy is:

1. **Aureon Zorza Technologies** — the research-led innovation company.
2. **Harmonic Nexus Core (HNC)** — the evolving research and systems framework.
3. **Aureon OS** — the company-built, publicly inspectable engineering and
   delivery environment.
4. **Mission blades** — sector-specific applications with their own evidence
   state and next proof.
5. **ORCID and Zenodo** — attribution and formal public research records.
6. **The Harmonic Nexus on Substack** — accessible explanation and working
   commentary, not formal validation.

## Positioning contract

Primary line:

> One research core. One delivery OS. Many specialised systems.

Supporting line:

> Research defines the principles. Aureon OS operationalises the discipline.
> Sector programmes test where it can create value.

Aureon's longer-horizon thesis may compare HNC with shared infrastructure that
supports many specialist machines. The public site must not say that HNC is a
new validated scientific field, a universal technology, or already deployed
across industries.

AI is an enabling technology within Aureon's system. It is not the company's
sole category or identity.

## Explicit public boundaries

- Research is not independent validation.
- Substack is explanation, not peer review.
- Source availability is not production readiness.
- Repository access is not verified use or adoption.
- A sector blade is an application thesis until its own proof closes.
- HNC is an evolving research and systems framework, not a proven universal
  scientific field.
- No raise, valuation, revenue, runway, grant, application, correspondence,
  portal, operator, hosting, or deployment records enter the public release.
