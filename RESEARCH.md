Design Aureon as an institutional research publisher with a disciplined evidence system: lead with ambitious, source-linked questions and article-specific editorial imagery, then make every claim state, open test and human decision easy to inspect.

# Aureon website research and visual direction

Research refreshed 26 July 2026. This file records the design and content evidence used for the website release. Public examples link to first-party pages. Authenticated company material is summarised without publishing private correspondence.

## Strategic position

Aureon should not resemble a generic AI launch page. Its strongest defensible position is an evidence and control layer for work that must remain inspectable, local-first and human-gated.

The public experience should answer five questions in order:

1. What is Aureon and which decision does it help control?
2. What source supports each material claim?
3. What is implemented, recorded, confirmed, independently validated or still to verify?
4. Where does human authority stop or approve an action?
5. What dated evidence receipt can a reviewer inspect?

This creates one primary journey: **Company → Platform → Evidence → Engage**. The header should remain compact; secondary material such as Vision, Live evidence and Community belongs in contextual routes and the footer.

## Sector references

### Product and infrastructure

- [Linear](https://linear.app/) makes real product state the visual story. Issue identifiers, owners, labels and status changes form a clear Intake → Plan → Build → Review → Monitor sequence. Aureon should borrow the legibility of this sequence, not its exact aesthetic.
- [Stripe](https://stripe.com/) pairs a direct proposition with hard, attributable proof and named customer outcomes. Aureon should use measured proof only when a source, date and boundary can be displayed.
- [Vercel](https://vercel.com/) uses high contrast, console artefacts and bounded deployment scenarios. Its useful motion explains a state change—loading, building or shipping—rather than decorating the page.
- [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/) presents itself as an institution through short copy, generous space and first-class routes to research, policy and trust material. This is a useful model for Aureon's research and diligence hierarchy.
- [Palantir Foundry](https://www.palantir.com/docs/foundry/platform-overview/overview) explains a complex system through objects, relationships, actions, security and governance. Aureon's evidence path should likewise show causal relationships rather than an abstract network.
- [Datadog](https://www.datadoghq.com/) uses genuine dashboards, traces and charts as visual identity. It proves the value of real interface evidence, while also showing how product breadth can overwhelm a first-time reviewer.
- [Snyk](https://snyk.io/) names a specific system, structures the problem into bounded threats and attaches outcomes to recognised organisations. Its specificity is stronger than generic “trusted AI” language.

### Assurance and AI governance

- [Vanta](https://www.vanta.com/) has an efficient first-screen trust stack: a short proposition, product interface, customer evidence, measured outcomes, framework coverage and buyer-stage segmentation.
- [Credo AI](https://www.credo.ai/) makes inventory → risk intelligence → policy engine visible. The mechanism is useful; repeated “agentic”, “trusted” and “continuous” wording is not.
- [Holistic AI](https://www.holisticai.com/) uses Identify → Protect → Enforce, backed by risk states, tests, gates, approvals and kill switches. This reinforces Aureon's decision to make state and authority visible.
- [IBM watsonx.governance](https://www.ibm.com/products/watsonx-governance) organises enterprise density into Visibility → Control → Accountability, supported by product screens, tabs, pricing and case studies.
- [Monitaur](https://www.monitaur.ai/) reduces its proposition to a policy-to-proof journey: Define → Manage → Automate. Its regulated-domain specificity is credible, although the public product evidence remains limited.
- [Fairly AI](https://fairly.ai/) now redirects to Asenion. Assess → Test → Assure and the public regulation tracker remain useful patterns. The redirect also demonstrates why stale names, template debris and migration inconsistencies are serious trust failures.

### Adjacent references

- [Drata](https://drata.com/) connects capabilities to measured audit and document outcomes and exposes a Trust Center. Placeholder-looking metrics demonstrate how quickly weak evidence can undermine the same page.
- [ModelOp](https://www.modelop.com/) presents one system of record that sits above rather than replaces the existing stack. Its architecture and role-specific routes are particularly relevant to Aureon.
- [OneTrust AI Governance](https://www.onetrust.com/solutions/ai-governance/) uses precise Catalog → Monitor → Enforce product language, names integrations and shows runtime enforcement.
- [Arize](https://arize.com/) frames its developer journey as Trace → Eval → Learn, then supports it with open-source code, open standards, self-hosting and documentation.
- [Fiddler](https://www.fiddler.ai/) visualises the request/response enforcement point and names what is blocked. This is more credible than an ungrounded “control plane” diagram.

## Patterns to use

- A warm editorial canvas with ink typography, documentary rules and one restrained navy evidence band.
- Gold for orientation and primary action.
- Teal only for a genuinely verified state.
- Article-specific research imagery attached to its exact title, source and evidence boundary.
- Real identifiers, sources, dates, owners, blockers and review states.
- Short propositions followed by inspectable proof.
- Architecture that shows what Aureon connects and what it does not replace.
- Motion that explains a state transition in 400ms or less.
- Clear routes to company identity, security boundaries, status, research, documentation and contact.
- Measured metrics with denominator, date, method and source.
- A public evidence vocabulary that prevents a company-recorded fact from appearing independently validated.

## Patterns to reject

- Generic “trusted”, “continuous”, “end-to-end”, “at scale” and “agentic” claims.
- Glowing spheres, orbit motifs and ambient networks that do not show provenance or authority.
- Logo walls, analyst badges and screenshots used as substitutes for evidence.
- Green “compliant” labels that collapse distinct evidence states.
- Unattributed numbers or performance claims.
- Marquees, parallax, scroll-jacking and long decorative loops.
- Stale dates, old names, template text and duplicated migration routes.
- Navigation so broad that a reviewer cannot find identity, deployment boundaries or current status.

## Diligence-reader requirements

The [NCSC vendor assessment guidance](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/report/vendor-security-assessment) distinguishes vendor evidence, testing that validates vendor claims and third-party evidence. NCSC [service-assessment guidance](https://www.ncsc.gov.uk/collection/zero-trust/zero-trust-network-access-ztna/what-to-do-before-building-a-ztna-architecture) also prioritises architecture, integrations, dependencies, logging, incident support and shared responsibility. [NPSA supply-chain guidance](https://www.npsa.gov.uk/security-best-practices/supply-chain-guidance/guidance-practitioners) adds financial, reputational, reliability and security risk.

The public diligence route should therefore expose:

- the controlled decision and intended reviewer;
- local, cloud and data boundaries plus shared responsibilities;
- architecture, integrations, source lineage and human-gate behaviour;
- claim state, accountable owner, source, checked date and validation level;
- security, audit logging, incident and dependency information;
- legal identity, leadership, change record, live status and a diligence contact.

## Grant-reader requirements

[UKRI assessment criteria](https://www.ukri.org/councils/stfc/guidance-for-applicants/what-happens-after-you-submit-your-proposal/review-and-assessment-of-proposals/assessment-criteria/) centre on vision, approach, team capability, resources, cost justification and responsible innovation. [Innovate UK general guidance](https://www.ukri.org/councils/innovate-uk/guidance-for-applicants/general-guidance/before-you-start-an-application/) requires eligibility, scope, complete answers and professional language. [Smart Grants guidance](https://www.ukri.org/councils/innovate-uk/guidance-for-applicants/guidance-for-specific-funds/smart-innovation-funding-guidance/) adds market need, exploitation and the reason public support matters.

The funding route should make scope, innovation, market need, impact, delivery, risk, team, resources and responsible-research evidence easy to inspect. A submitted application must not be presented as an award.

The commercial sequence remains a hypothesis to validate: evidence packs and implementation work → reusable controls and templates → a productised Evidence OS.

## Research publishing and artwork

The authenticated mailbox contains Substack shareable-asset notices for the Harmonic Nexus research index and individual articles. The public Substack archive returned 23 current entries on 26 July 2026; the site catalogue also retains one separately reachable direct note with that distinction visible.

The strongest editorial mechanism is the pairing of a provocative research question with an explicit test boundary:

- falsifiability, reproducibility and null models;
- AI behaviour and data-led observatories;
- early-stage photonics, propulsion and protection architectures;
- space, sensing and non-detection;
- geology, bio-energy and historical synthesis.

Selected Substack artwork may provide the visual energy of the site only when it is paired with the exact author-published note. It is editorial illustration, not a photograph of company facilities, a validated experiment or proof of capability. The visual should invite the question; the adjacent copy must state what would test it.

## Release design decisions

- Maintain one canonical six-link header: Platform, Research, Evidence, Diligence, Company and Open diligence. The brand is the Home link.
- Make Research the first narrative after the legal and public-record proof strip; keep Funding and Contact as contextual routes rather than primary navigation.
- Introduce a governed `tokens.css` for colour, claim state, type, spacing, radius, elevation and motion.
- Use self-hosted WOFF2 fonts and no third-party font CDN.
- Use article-specific responsive WebP artwork in a restrained editorial research gallery, always with intrinsic dimensions and a source-linked caption.
- Enhance the evidence path into keyboard tabs only after JavaScript loads; every stage remains readable without JavaScript.
- Use an accessible mobile navigation disclosure with Escape handling and a visible focus ring.
- Enforce canonical HTTPS, conservative security headers, HTML revalidation, immutable static-asset caching and a branded 404 at the web-server boundary.

## Continuous-improvement test

Every future graphic, animation or page should pass this test:

> Does it help a reviewer understand a source, claim state, dependency, human gate or decision more accurately?

If not, it is decoration and should be removed or simplified.
