# Aureon Harmonic Design Suite

Build an institutional, research-led public website from current evidence. Use Harmonic Nexus as the shape of the feedback loop, never as automated aesthetic truth or scientific validation.

## Operating sequence

1. Locate the active Aureon repository. Preserve unrelated dirty state and runtime-only credentials.
2. Read `references/design-doctrine.md`, `references/harmonic-feedback-contract.md`, and `references/release-authority.md`.
3. Inventory the current `website/` tree, claims inputs, research catalogue, ORCID material, pitch evidence, and release state. Label facts, interpretations, hypotheses, and unavailable evidence.
4. Run the deterministic baseline:

   ```powershell
   python skills/aureon-harmonic-design-suite/scripts/run_design_cycle.py --goal "<bounded design objective>"
   ```

5. Run `python -m aureon.operator.design_research_refresh --repo-root .` before using volatile research, peer, ORCID or public-attention evidence in a candidate. It validates only redacted local public-source snapshots, schedules due observations, and never clears artwork, fetches a provider, or grants candidate/release authority. Refresh competitor evidence from official sources when it is older than the configured freshness window. Record URL, checked time, pattern evidence, and use boundary. Never copy source code, copy, imagery, or trade dress.
   Before translating stakeholder feedback, require a passing `aureon.operator.design_stakeholder_feedback` audit of the canonical human-created redacted declaration. Only the declaration binding, freshness, aggregate counts, deterministic capsule hashes, and exact controlled-code route capsule may cross into design planning. Raw correspondence, the human-redacted evidence snapshot, identities, quotations, provider metadata, URLs, finance, and free-form rationale never enter a worker context. This is planning evidence only and grants no candidate, package, release, connector, credential, or deployment authority.
6. Before selecting a baseline for a successor candidate, use `WebsiteOperator.observe_live_surface` or `aureon-website live-drift` on the relevant production routes. This is read-only drift sensing only: a matching receipt is not release authority, and a divergent or incomplete receipt requires a fresh verified backup plus an explicit owner-scoped source-reconciliation decision. The unchanged v1 decision may retain local canonical source. Only the exact v2 decision may select a fresh manifest-bound verified backup below `artifacts/homepl-backups/`; candidate control revalidates it byte by byte, rejects server-only paths and recognised credential patterns, and copies only public static files plus the intentional `.htaccess` into its sealed artifact workspace. Never infer a source, auto-copy live content into `website/`, or overwrite production from local source.

## Quality contract

Accept a local candidate only when:

- every hard gate passes under an unchanged test policy;
- every targeted finding is closed;
- no existing hard gate regresses;
- material claims are bound to current permitted evidence;
- the investor-copy quality audit passes against the exact selected source and staged candidate, with no static investor, internal-finance, research-total, traction-total, or KPI-counter figures;
- the editorial-asset provenance audit is current; the global artwork state remains `not-cleared`; every retained or added asset is bound to one exact candidate-use-ready route capsule backed by a separate controlled allowlisted named-owner-reviewer rights decision; and `candidate_use_rights_ready` is never relabelled as candidate asset readiness;
- every asset-bearing v4 candidate reaches `candidate-assets-ready` only through the runner after the trusted content-addressed importer completes the exact batch and its immutable receipt passes current byte, work-order, provenance, route and rights replay verification; text-only candidates never enter that state;
- final candidate validation replays structural public surfaces and asset references from staged bytes, permits only separately controlled text deltas, and proves every binary delta is exactly receipt-bound;
- any HNC evidence-control graph remains source-neutral until owner source selection, any required verified backup, a fresh hash-bound exact-path work order, and named human visual acceptance are all present;
- the current source-bound design-evidence brief passes, every selected route retains its exact permitted claim capsule and static/reduced-motion visual rule, and brief-ready is not treated as candidate-delivery-ready;
- the canonical stakeholder-feedback declaration is current and privacy-safe, every worker receives only the exact controlled-code route capsule, and the staged delivery contains a complete hash-bound response manifest that closes every sealed signal without expanding its path or claim scope;

- source, brief, policy, candidate, screenshot, and package hashes are recorded;
- every focused performance series is preceded by a source-bound initial gate that passes both deferred-render geometry and every fixed initial performance check;
- autonomous V30+ candidates have a reconciled v4 exact-path work order, current live-surface evidence, a fresh owner source-selection decision plus verified backup whenever production drift exists, source baseline, one consumed staged-worker lease where a worker is used, and one consumed V2 candidate-QA attempt bound to the unchanged candidate and canonical tree. ## Non-negotiable boundaries

- The public site is read-only; it never exposes operator, credential, trading, grant, filing, or client controls.
- The public narrative exists to attract qualified investors through the thesis, research authority, shared-core leverage, sector relevance, public proof, and next validation. It does not publish raise, valuation, revenue, runway, application, grant, provider-route, correspondence, meeting, portal, operator, deployment, or hosting records.
- Prefer flagship questions, methods, source routes, and attributable evidence over catalogue arithmetic or KPI counters. Public identifiers and dates may appear only when they help a reviewer reopen an authoritative source, such as Companies House, ORCID, Zenodo, or a DOI.
- Platform analytics may support a clearly dated public-attention signal only when the source, measurement window and interpretation boundary are preserved. Treat views, downloads, clones, stars and forks as different signals; never relabel them as citations, customers, production use, scientific validation or independent adoption.
- Keep each analytics window independent. Never sum rolling or overlapping windows, and never add window-distinct visitors or cloners into a cross-window "unique" total. A renamed repository may be presented as one history only after an authoritative redirect or rename receipt is preserved.
- Store visitor-level exports outside every public Git checkout. The release may use reviewed aggregates, hashes and date boundaries; it must not expose visitor identifiers, locations, referrers, search keywords or signed download links.
- Research may be the product story, but authored research, hypotheses, demonstrations, discussions, submissions, partners, customers, awards, validation, and financing are separate states.
- Motion must explain hierarchy or state, remain restrained, and preserve reduced-motion and keyboard parity.
- "Harmonic", "coherence", and "Design Nexus" are internal operating terms unless public wording is explicitly evidenced and appropriate.
- Local design readiness is not deployment readiness. Never say "live" without current production read-back.
- A live-surface reconciliation receipt is not a source-selection decision, canonical promotion, package, backup, owner approval or deployment authority. Drift is resolved only through a fresh verified backup and explicit owner-scoped reconciliation.
- A passing design-evidence brief is not a candidate, candidate-validation receipt, browser gate, visual acceptance, canonical promotion, package, backup, owner approval or deployment authority.
